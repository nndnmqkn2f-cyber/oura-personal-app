# Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

## What this app is

"Tom Training Partner" is a personal application used by one individual, its owner, to download his own Oura Ring data to his own computer. It is not offered to the public, has no other users, and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Oura.

## Data accessed

When the owner authorizes the app, it reads only the owner's own Oura data in these categories: daily summaries (activity, sleep and readiness), heart rate, workouts, stress, and heart health.

## How the data is used

The data is saved to files on the owner's personal computer and is used only by the owner for personal training and health analysis.

## Sharing

The data is not sold, shared, published or disclosed to any other person or service for advertising, analytics or any other purpose.

## Storage and retention

The data stays on the owner's personal computer until the owner deletes it. The app keeps no servers or databases.

## Access control

The owner can revoke the app's access to his Oura data at any time from his Oura account settings, and can delete the saved files at any time.
